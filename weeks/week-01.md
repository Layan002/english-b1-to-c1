## 🛠️ Before you start (one time only)
| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Install **Anki** (flashcards) | PC: https://apps.ankiweb.net · Android: search **"AnkiDroid"** in Google Play · iPhone: **"AnkiMobile"** (paid) — or use https://ankiweb.net in the browser (free) |
| Make one Anki deck called **English B1→C1** | Card front: the expression · Card back: meaning + your own example sentence |
| Get the books (PDF or paper) | Search: **"English Grammar in Use Intermediate Raymond Murphy"** and **"English Vocabulary in Use Upper-Intermediate McCarthy O'Dell"** (Jarir Bookstore has them) |
