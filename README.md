# 🇬🇧 English B1 → C1 in 6 Months

**Start:** 22 Nov 2026 · **End:** 22 May 2027 · **Daily session:** 8:00–9:30 PM (Google Calendar) · **Time:** 90 min/day, 6 days/week (Sun–Fri) (≈ 230 hours)

> Honest note: B1 → C1 normally takes 400–600 hours. 6 months is possible only with daily work **plus** a lot of free English (movies, YouTube, podcasts) in your free time. Aim for C1 in reading/listening and a strong B2+/C1 in speaking/writing.

## 🇨🇦 Accent rule
Target accent: **Canadian / North American**.
- **Shadow only North American speakers:** Friends, Kim's Convenience, Schitt's Creek, Rachel's English, CBC.
- **BBC 6 Minute English = listening + vocabulary only** (never shadow it). Understanding British English is still useful (IELTS, people in Canada).

## ⏰ Daily routine (90 min)

| Min | Activity |
|---|---|
| 20 | Grammar **or** Vocabulary in Use (alternate days) |
| 20 | Listening / watching (podcast or 1 episode part) — write down 5 new expressions |
| 10 | Shadowing — repeat 5–10 lines out loud, copy the rhythm (**North American speakers only**) |
| 20 | Speaking with Claude **or** writing (Write & Improve) |
| 10 | Reading (1 article) |
| 10 | Anki review |

**Free time:** extra episodes, YouTube in English, change phone language to English.
**Saturday:** catch-up day — do only the tasks you missed this week (max 60 min), then rest. Nothing missed? Just rest or watch something fun.

---

## 🎯 Phases

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Goal at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Foundations | 1–8 | Fix grammar gaps, core B2 vocabulary, listening with transcripts | Comfortable B2: understand 6 Minute English without transcript |
| 2 — Expansion | 9–17 | Advanced grammar, phrasal verbs & collocations, role-plays, longer writing | B2+: follow sitcoms with English subtitles, write 250-word essays |
| 3 — Fluency | 18–26 | Speaking without pausing, no subtitles, IELTS-style tests | C1: understand TED/news without subtitles, speak 2 min on any topic |
